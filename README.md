**Portfolio projects** (each has a repo, a one-page write-up and a Tableau dashboard)

- [Premium-screen investment model](https://github.com/Saathvik007/premium-screen-investment-model): a 10-year Excel DCF built from AMC and IMAX 10-K figures. Converting a standard screen to premium large format has a base-case NPV of -$213,905 and only breaks even at a 115.7% attendance uplift.
- [E-commerce operations analysis](https://github.com/Saathvik007/ecommerce-operations-analysis): SQL analysis of 96,470 delivered Olist orders. Late orders averaged 2.27 review stars, against 4.29 for on-time orders.
- [Event check-in system case](https://github.com/Saathvik007/event-checkin-system-case): requirements, process maps, a SQLite prototype and 18 passing acceptance tests for a made-up career office, built on synthetic data.

**Course projects at NYU**

- [Beyond the Firewall](https://github.com/Saathvik007/cybersecurity-industry-analysis): a five-person team paper on how AI and vendor consolidation are changing the US cybersecurity industry. I wrote the Business Model Canvas and SWOT of Palo Alto Networks.
- [Strategy briefs on WSJ deals](https://github.com/Saathvik007/wsj-strategy-briefs): Compass buying Anywhere for $1.6 billion, JPMorgan's $10 billion security investment plan and Google's $24 billion AI build-out.
